# Revised SOP
## Current Process
1. Worker informs team lead of change request.
2. Team lead approves and implements in operational equipment.
3. If issues arise, revert to previous state.

## Policy Requirements
- All changes require a ticket number.
- Separation of workers and approvers for general changes.
- Backup and recovery testing confirmation before implementation.
- Immediate rollback if failure criteria met.
- Emergency changes may be prioritized but must receive approval within 24 hours.

## Request Details
- A writable SOP for field workers.
- Clear step-by-step checklist with distinction between general and emergency changes.
