# Decision Recommendation: AI Inspection System Investment

## Conclusion
**Conditional Approval**
Proceed with the investment in the AI inspection system, but only after validating its effectiveness across all six production lines. This decision is based on promising pilot results and significant cost savings potential.

---

## Key Evidence
### Financial Impact
- **Investment Cost**: ₩4.2 billion (one-time).
- **Annual Maintenance Cost**: ₩0.6 billion.
- **Current Annual Defect Loss**: ₩3.0 billion.
- **Pilot Results**: 22% reduction in defect losses during the 8-week trial.

### Operational Impact
- **Inspection Time Reduction**: 14% faster during the pilot (Line 1 only).
- **Scalability Unconfirmed**: Pilot was conducted on only one of six production lines. Further validation is required to ensure consistent results across all lines.

---

## Risks and Uncertainties
1. **Limited Pilot Scope**: The pilot was conducted on a single line, and the effectiveness on other lines has not been validated.
2. **Potential Overestimation**: Extrapolating pilot results to the entire plant may lead to overestimating cost savings and operational benefits.
3. **Implementation Challenges**: Integration with existing systems and workforce training may introduce delays or additional costs.
